🔒 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Vehicle Manager. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all personal and vehicle-related information stored within the application.

Core Guarantee: Vehicle Manager is designed around a Privacy-First Architecture. Your vehicle logs, service records, fuel costs, and odometer readings are stored locally on your device.

📊 2. Data We Collect & Store

When you use Vehicle Manager, the following data is captured strictly for the functionality of tracking your fleet:

  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle Name, Type (Car, Bike, SUV, Auto, Truck), Registration Number, Fuel Type, and Initial Odometer Reading.
  • Transaction Records: Fuel costs, service bills, maintenance expenses, income logs, payment modes, and notes.
  • Service Reminders: Custom reminder titles, due dates, and target odometer thresholds.
  • App Preferences: Selected currency symbol (₹, $, €, £) and distance unit (km/mi).

💾 3. Local Storage & Security

All application data is written locally to an encrypted SQLite database located in your device's secure app sandbox. We do not operate remote servers that track your daily vehicle movements or log your private expenses.

🔔 4. Device Permissions & Notifications

To deliver timely maintenance reminders, Vehicle Manager requests explicit permission to send device notifications. The app uses these permissions strictly for user-requested vehicle alerts:

  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) and iOS to display service due alerts, insurance renewal notices, and fuel log reminders in your device's notification tray.
  • Exact Alarm Permission (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to schedule precise alerts at your specified date and time without battery drain or background location tracking.
  • File Storage / Document Picker: Requested only when you manually initiate a database export or restore from Settings > Data & Backup.
Zero Spam & Zero Tracking Policy: Notification permissions are used exclusively for local, user-scheduled vehicle alerts. Vehicle Manager will never send promotional messages, ads, or background tracking alerts.

📤 5. Database Export & Data Portability

You retain full control over your data. You can export a full .db file backup at any time from Settings > Data & Backup and share or store it safely wherever you prefer.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or app security, please reach out via our official support channel.
